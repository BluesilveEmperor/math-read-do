# Nature Communications formatting reference

## Article types

| Type | Max refs | Abstract | Main text limit |
|---|---|---|---|
| Article | 70 | ~150 words | ~5000 words |
| Review | 200 | ~150 words | ~8000 words |
| Perspective | 100 | ~150 words | ~5000 words |
| Comment | 20 | none | ~1000 words |
| Correspondence | 10 | none | ~500 words |

## Article structure (standard)

1. Introduction (no separate heading)
2. Results (with subsection headings)
3. Discussion (no separate heading for short articles)
4. Methods (at end, separate from main text)
5. Data availability
6. Code availability
7. References
8. Acknowledgements
9. Author contributions
10. Competing interests

## Figure and table limits

- Main figures: up to 10 for Articles, up to 6 for Reviews.
- Main tables: up to 5.
- Supplementary: no fixed limit but should be selective.
- Each main figure can have up to 10 panels (a–j).

## Reference style

- Numbered sequentially in order of appearance.
- In text: superscript Arabic numerals (e.g., `¹` or `(1)`).
- Authors: up to 5 before `et al.`; initials after surname: `Smith, J. A. et al.`
- Journal abbreviations per Index Medicus.
